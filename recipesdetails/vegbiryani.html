Veg Biryani

Description

Vegetable Biryani is an aromatic rice dish made with basmati rice, mixed vegetables, herbs, and biryani spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 tablespoons ghee or vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 green cardamom pods
  • 3 cloves
  • 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • 1/2 cup carrots, diced
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1/2 cup potatoes, diced
  • 1/2 cup cauliflower florets
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 3 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons chopped coriander
  • 2 tablespoons chopped mint leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon saffron soaked in 2 tablespoons warm milk

Directions

  1. Rinse the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for 20-30 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat ghee or oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add cumin seeds, bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves. Fry for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the sliced onion and cook until golden brown. Add ginger garlic paste and green chili, then cook for another 1-2 minutes.
  4. Add the chopped tomato and cook until it becomes soft. Stir in turmeric powder, red chili powder, cumin powder, garam masala, and salt.
  5. Add the carrots, peas, potatoes, and cauliflower. Mix well and cook for about 5 minutes.
  6. Stir in the yogurt and cook the vegetables for another 3-4 minutes.
  7. Add the drained basmati rice and gently mix it with the vegetables. Pour in the water and bring everything to a boil.
  8. Reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and cook for about 15-20 minutes, or until the rice is completely cooked and the water has been absorbed.
  9. Drizzle the saffron milk over the biryani. Sprinkle with chopped coriander and mint leaves. Cover and let it rest for 5-10 minutes.
  10. Gently fluff the biryani with a fork and serve hot.
